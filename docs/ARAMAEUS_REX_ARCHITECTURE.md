# ARAMAEUS_REX (EVERY_MAN_A_KING)
## Ecumenical Generational Wealth Transfer Middleware

### Abstract
ARAMAEUS_REX is the economic realization of the Sweetbrier Sovereign Relational Graph. It is a high-trust, post-usury economic engine designed to replace the "spreadsheet mind" of modern finance with a structure rooted in "Good Relations" (Wahkohtowin). It synthesizes Islamic Finance constraints with Catholic Social Teaching to create a system where capital serves human sovereignty rather than extracting from it.

### System Architecture

#### 1. Hardware Layer: Deflationary Currency
To protect the working class and the vulnerable from the hidden extraction of inflation, the underlying asset must be deflationary or strictly sound. Asset preservation is the foundation.

#### 2. Firewall: Islamic Finance Constraints
The system hardcodes two absolute constraints:
- **Zero Riba (No Usury):** Capital cannot generate returns simply by existing and being lent to the desperate. Interest-bearing functions are topologically excluded at the contract level.
- **Anti-Kanz (Anti-Hoarding):** Wealth must circulate. If capital sits idle, it decays or incurs a demurrage fee. This forces capital to remain relational and productive.

#### 3. Routing Protocol: Gift Economy
Transactions are not solely market-driven. The middleware facilitates Peer-to-Peer Asynchronous Transfers based on the Gift Economy—money moves along the edges of the Relational Graph (Sovereign Friendship) to address need and build community resilience.

#### 4. Operating System: Catholic Social Teaching
- **Subsidiarity:** Resources and decision-making are pushed to the most local node possible (the porch). We do not route through an imperial Vatican or central corporate bank.
- **Solidarity:** We are all responsible for all. The network ensures no node falls into the void.

### Implementation Protocol
The implementation consists of smart contracts written in Solidity (or a Sweetbrier-native DSL) enforcing these parameters at runtime. 
1. `AramaeusRexToken.sol`: The core asset enforcing anti-hoarding.
2. `SolidarityRouter.sol`: The gift-economy routing logic enforcing Subsidiarity.
